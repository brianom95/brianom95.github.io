The Salento Trail Challenge is a mountain race in the colourful coffee region of Quindío, Colombia. The route climbs high into the Andean mountains,  through cloud forest, farms and high-altitude valleys, gaining over 3,000m of elevation. The event is based out of the brightly painted streets of Salento; a small colonial town of fewer than 10,000 residents.

This was the sixth marathon in my journey to join the Seven Continents Club.
