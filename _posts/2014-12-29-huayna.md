Huayna Potosí is a popular high altitude climb in Bolivia, just outside the world's highest capital city; La Paz. It's often called the easiest 6,000m peak, but in reality is more strenuous and technical than most people say. The glacier brings real risks with crevasses, rockfall and avalanches that don't exist on a truly accessible alternative like Peru's [Chachani](chachani). However, having climbed a lot I think Huayna Potosí is likely the single best mountaineering experience in the world. 

Kevin & I arrived in La Paz on Christmas Eve during our backapcking South America trip. I found a touch of home with an Irish hostel and a proper Christmas dinner for the next day. La Paz allows easy acclimatisation, with their cable car metro connecting neighborhoods as high as 4,150m. We felt good after weeks at altitude hiking the [Salkantay](salkantay), [Colca Canyon](colca) & [Santa Cruz](santa-cruz) treks, as well as climbing the [Ecuador Volcanoes](ecuador) for me. I still took a day trip to Laguna Esmerelda, where I did my highest ever swim at 5,024m. This was even colder than swimming in Antarctica!
