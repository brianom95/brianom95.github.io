Arequipa is a beautiful town that's famous for its many architectural styles. These are the result of centuries of earthquakes

Chachani is a high-altitude peak in the Andes near Arequipa, Peru. non-technical offering a challenging acclimatisation climb with expansive desert and volcanic views from its broad summit.
