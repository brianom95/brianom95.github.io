Huayna Potosí is a popular high altitude climb in Bolivia, just outside the world's highest capital city; La Paz. It's often called the easiest 6,000m peak, but in reality is more strenuous and technical than most people say. The glacier brings real risks with crevasses, rockfall and avalanches that don't exist on a truly accessible alternative like Peru's [Chachani](chachani). However, having climbed it I can say that Huayna Potosí is the single best mountaineering experience in the world. 

La Paz allows easy acclimatisation, with their cable car metro connecting neighborhoods as high as 4,150m. Kevin & I arrived just in time for Christmas 2024 while backapcking South America.
