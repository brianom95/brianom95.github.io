The Santa Cruz Trek is one of South America’s classic high-altitude hikes, through the Cordillera Blanca range near Huaraz, Peru. It spans 63km of remote valleys, glacial lakes, snow-capped peaks, and crosses the dramatic Punta Unión Pass at 4,750m. It also passes under Artesonraju Mountain (6,025m), known for its use in the Paramount Logo. The hike typically takes four days, but three worked fine for me as I'll describe below.

Aldo's Guest House is the best hostel in Huaraz, mostly because of Aldo. He's a local climber who can advise on any mountain or trek. He recommended a guide service that was €90 with portered tents, food, and shuttles either side. It was better value than renting a tent and going solo, and he explained how I could finish it a day early. To top it off he loaned me a big sleeping bag, rain pants and rucksack for free to make sure I was warm enough. Great guy. 

The next morning was the horribly uncomfortable bus to the trail head, up zig-zagging gravel roads for six hours. There was a nice stop at Lagunas del Valle Llanganuco (3,850m) which was incredibly blue, then down the zig-zags on the other side. We'd walk back the length of the range so had good roads for the way home, but getting to this side now was rough. At the trailhead we did introductions with our guide Cesar, chef Jesus, our team of porter donkeys, and the clients; Rory, Eva, William, Etienne, Phoebe & Eva.
