The Santa Cruz Trek is one of South America’s classic high-altitude hikes, through the Cordillera Blanca range near Huaraz, Peru. It spans 63km of remote valleys, glacial lakes, snow-capped peaks, and crosses the dramatic Punta Unión Pass at 4,750m. It also passes under Artesonraju Mountain (6,025m), known for its use in the Paramount Logo. The hike typically takes four days, but three worked fine for me as I'll describe below.

Aldo's Guest House is the best hostel in Huaraz, mostly because of Aldo. He's a local climber who can advise on any mountain or trek. He recommended a guide service that was €90 with portered tents, food, and shuttles either side. It was better value than renting a tent and going solo, and he explained how I could hike more to finish it a day early. To top it off he loaned me a big sleeping bag, rain pants and rucksack for free to make sure I was warm enough. Great guy.

#### Day 1 - 11.17km (424m)

The next morning was the uncomfortable bus to the trail head, up zig-zagging gravel roads for four hours to our lunch break. We stopped again at Lagunas del Valle Llanganuco (3,850m) which was incredibly blue, then down the zig-zags on the other side for another three hours. We'd walk back over the whole range so had short roads for the way home, but getting to this side of the mountains was rough. At the trailhead we did introductions with our guide Cesar, chef Jesus, our team of porter donkeys, and the fun clients; Rory, Eva, William, Etienne, Phoebe & Eva.

The donkeys carried the team gear and food, and once loaded and gone we followed. It was a beautiful sunny day, following a small stream through grassy fields into the valley. Lots of sheep and old stone walls until we reached a ranger station to sign into the national park, then it became much more remote mountain. Our camp was in a scenic spot below our next pass, and the food was hearty and filling. It was loud by the stream but the stars were amazing. I was glad to be here with this group, having laughed and joked the whole day to here.
