## What Is A Country?

Any area with a permanent population, defined borders and its own government can claim independence. The mark of a country requires international recognition of this sovereignty, usually done through a majority vote within the United Nations (UN). The most recent to do this being South Sudan in 2011, making it the youngest country.

The UN currently recognises 193 countries, plus 2 observer states (Palestine & Vatican City). The other 2 states that we include in the list are Kosovo (recognized by 102 UN members) and Taiwan (recognized by 12 UN members and one UN observer).
