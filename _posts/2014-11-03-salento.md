*The Salento Trail Challenge is a mountain race in the colourful coffee region of Quindío, Colombia. The route climbs high into the Andean mountains,  through cloud forest, farms and high-altitude valleys, gaining over 3,000m of elevation. The event is based out of the brightly painted streets of Salento; a small colonial town of 10,000 residents.*

This was the sixth marathon in my journey to join the Seven Continents Club.

This race was done during my six months of Backpacking South America, suiting the route and timeframe of that trip. I raced the full while Mairead did the 10K, and we treated ourselves to a fancy B&B with a jacuzzi for the occasion. The expo was simple but fun, collecting t-shirts and race numbers in the main square. The valley is cold, green, and feels like high altitude. We had a big dinner the night before with friends of mine from the Colombia sailing trip.

There were about fifty of us lined up at 6am to start.
